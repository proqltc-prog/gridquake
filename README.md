# Gridquake: Block Puzzle

Block-puzzle game for smartphones (`be.proqltc.gridquake`). It is built the same way as Seed & Destroy: the game is one web page (`index.html`), and Capacitor wraps it into an Android app.

## Test version on your phone
A test APK is only built on request: run **Android app** by hand (Actions → Android app → Run workflow), only after the owner approved it. Until then the game is tested in the web version. To install an APK, open the run in the **Actions** tab, download the artifact `gridquake-apk`, unzip it and install `app-debug.apk` on your Android phone. Every test build is signed with the same test key, so a new version installs over the old one.

## Google Play version
1. The upload key is made on the owner's PC and kept in `C:\Dev\Gridquake\sleutels` (never in the repository, OneDrive or a chat). Its contents go into the GitHub secrets `ANDROID_UPLOAD_KEYSTORE` (from `upload.keystore.base64.txt`) and `ANDROID_UPLOAD_PASSWORD` (from `wachtwoord.txt`), entered by the owner on the GitHub website. Key alias: `upload`.
2. Publish a release, or run **Play Store build** by hand. The artifact `gridquake-play` holds the signed `.aab` for Google Play.

## Status
- Campaign: 11 maps of 20 levels (stones, ice, chains, moss, time bombs, islands, paint, keys and chests, rising lava, crystals, mixed). A map opens after its boss (level 20). Each map has 3 goals with bronze, silver and gold medals. Moves and star limits are tuned with a bot that plays every level 24 times.
- Score: 10 points per placed cell, 200 for one line (more for several lines at once), a streak of clearing moves multiplies by 2, 3, 4…; 1,000 per move left in a level. Big numbers use the thousands separator of the language. Version 0.6 converted older scores once (x10, on the device and on the server, migration `gq05`).
- Classic (0.7): plain block puzzle without power blocks.
- End card (0.7), in Classic and for a lost level, always 4 choices: play on with a heart (once per game), play on after a rewarded video (once per game), play again, menu. Playing on gives 3 new blocks that fit (Classic or a full board), +5 moves, +5 on every time bomb, or cools the lava.
- Combo fire (0.7): when the combo reaches 8 (then 16, 24...) the combo bar burns, the announcer shouts FRENZY! (UNREAL! at 16, GODLIKE! at 24) with fireworks and its own sound, and the player gets one free Rocket with the helpers under the tray. It is only for that game: used before bought rockets, needs no server, gone when the game ends and never added to the wallet.
- Announcer (0.7): short English shouts in every language, arena style: DOUBLE!, CROSSFIRE!, MULTI CLEAR! (3 lines), MEGA CLEAR! (4+), CHAIN REACTION!, ULTRA! (a huge move), FLAWLESS! (empty board), and a combo ladder SPREE! (3), RAMPAGE!, DOMINATING!, UNSTOPPABLE!, MONSTER! (7).
- Power combo (0.7): two power blocks side by side go off together as one bigger blast (Campaign and duels).
- Coins, shop and Wheel of Luck (0.7): coins are earned by playing (level win 10 + 5 per star, Classic up to 30) and spent in the shop (green Buy button with the price; the owned count sits on the icon and goes up right away) on helpers: hammer, turn, new blocks, rocket (tap an empty cell; also as Starter Rocket before a level) and the heart (play on at the end of a game). A gift box after every 3rd level, a free Starter Rocket after 3 wins in a row (kept on the device). During a game the four helpers stand in one row under the blocks; a helper the player has none of is grey and cannot be used. The heart is a counter, not a helper: shown next to the coins in the menu and in the combo line during a game, used on the end card, and listed apart under "Hearts" in the shop. No real-money purchases; helpers never work in duels.
- Server wallet (0.7): coins, helpers, the daily wheel and the video bonuses are kept and checked on the server, in schema `gridquake` (migration `gq09`, functions `gridquake.wallet`, `buy`, `use_item`, `wheel_spin`, `ad_claim`, `earn_level`, `earn_classic`). The app calls them with the header `Content-Profile: gridquake`, so `gridquake` must be listed under Exposed schemas (Data API). Without a connection the wallet is closed.
  - Wheel of Luck ("Rad van Fortuin"): one free spin a day, the day changes at midnight Europe/Brussels. After it, one extra spin a day for a rewarded video.
  - Coin video next to the coins: +100 coins, once every 30 minutes, at most 10 a day.
  - Videos count only when Google confirms them: AdMob server-side verification calls the Edge Function `gq-admob-ssv` (signature check, tested with `node tests/ssv.test.mjs`), which pays through `gridquake.record_ssv`. Test builds use Google's test ads, which send no confirmation; until the date in `gridquake.settings.unverified_until` (set to 4 Dec 2026 by migration `gq11`) the app may claim the reward itself, with the same limits; after that date only confirmed videos pay. Before the Play Store release: add the SSV callback URL `https://cmmgsnkvqcvjsxldflvo.supabase.co/functions/v1/gq-admob-ssv` to the Gridquake rewarded ad unit in AdMob and end test mode with `update gridquake.settings set value = 'null' where key = 'unverified_until';`.
  - Limits against scripted claims (`gq11`): a first level win pays at most once every 20 seconds and for 40 levels a day (the gift box only with a paid first win); a new Classic game pays at most once a minute and 300 coins a day.
- Username (0.7): chosen once at the bottom of the menu and never changed afterwards (migration `gq10`). 3-16 characters, Latin letters (also with accents), digits, one space, - or _ between them, at least 2 letters; rude (also "l33t"), reserved and look-alike names are refused on the server.
- Campaign map (0.7): friends are shown on the level they are playing (`gq_friends` returns `lvl`, migration `gq07`).
- Crystal Mine (0.7): new map 10 ("Kristalmijn", levels 181-200) before the master test, which is now map 11 (levels 201-220, same layouts and tuning as before). Crystals count as filled for a line but are never cleared; after every move they fall through empty cells, and in the bottom row they are collected (goal: bring all crystals down). Tuned with `scripts/tune-bot.mjs` (calibrated on maps 1-4): `node scripts/tune-bot.mjs verify 181-200` checks it. Server limits widened to 220 levels and 11 maps (migration `gq08`).
- First start (0.7): straight into the first guided exercise; the language follows the phone.
- Themes (0.7), all free: Light and Dark are calm (no moving background, no combo glow); Sunset, Deep sea, Space and Neon night have their own colours, a softly moving background (light motes, bubbles, stars with shooting stars, neon squares) and a glow that grows with the combo. New players start with Sunset; nothing moves when the phone asks for reduced motion. Chosen in Settings with a small preview of each theme.
- Screen layout (0.7): in the menu the title stands alone; coins, hearts, the coin video and the round buttons for wheel, shop and settings share one row. During a game every row (score, goals, combo line, tray, helpers, hint) is exactly as wide as the board frame; the combo number only shows during a combo. There is no separate sound button: sound and vibration are switched in Settings ("Audio en trillingen").
- Settings: language, theme, sound effects and vibration switches, recovery code, ad privacy, delete.
- First start: a 4-screen guide on how the game works. The campaign starts with a practice map of 5 guided levels (place blocks, Rocket, using a Rocket, Bomb, Prism) with blocks on the board, lit target cells and an explanation above the board; map 1 opens after it. Classic has no tutorial.
- Online: leaderboards (world, week, friends), friends, duels (random and friend), campaign stars, recovery code and account deletion run on the Supabase functions `gq_*` (project seed-and-destroy, schema `gridquake`), with an anonymous login per device, the same way as Seed & Destroy. Without a connection, Classic and Campaign keep working.
- Ads: AdMob rewarded video (+5 moves, clear the board, continue) and an interstitial after every 5th game, with Google's EU consent form and a privacy-options button in Settings when Google requires it. Test APKs always use Google's test ads; only the Play Store build (`npm run android -- --release`) switches on live ads and the live app id. In a browser a short stand-in replaces the ad.
- Icon and splash screen: own design (blocks blown apart by a bomb), made with `scripts/make-icons.cjs` into `android-assets/res/`. Store icon and feature graphic in `store/`.
- iOS: not yet (Apple Developer account needed first).
