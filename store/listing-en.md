## Full description (max 4000)
Gridquake is a block puzzle with a bite. Drag blocks onto an 8×8 board, fill rows and columns, and watch them blow apart. Clear lines move after move and your combo climbs — hit the top of the bar and the board catches fire.

CAMPAIGN: 220 LEVELS ON 11 MAPS
Every map brings its own trouble: stones that take several hits, ice, chains, creeping moss, time bombs, islands of water, paint, keys and chests, rising lava and falling crystals — and a master test that throws it all at you. Some levels start with a board already full of blocks in a shape, others run against the clock, and from map 6 you race both moves and time. Earn up to three stars per level and bronze, silver and gold medals per map.

POWER BLOCKS
Clear two lines at once and you forge a Rocket. Three at once gives a Bomb. A full line in one colour makes a Prism. Line two power blocks up side by side and they go off together.

CLASSIC
No limits, no goals: keep placing blocks until the board is full and chase your record.

3-MINUTE DUELS
Challenge a random player or a friend. You both get exactly the same blocks; after three minutes the higher score wins. A live bar shows how your opponent did at the same moment.

HELPERS, COINS AND THE WHEEL OF LUCK
Smash a cell with the Hammer, turn a block, swap your blocks for new ones or drop a Rocket where you need it. Earn coins by winning levels and playing Classic, spin the Wheel of Luck every day and open a gift every third level. A heart lets you play on once per game.

THEMES
Light, Disco, Sunset, Deep sea, Space and Neon night — the lively ones move, glow and react to your combos. All themes are free.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Short levels, quick to pick up
• Leaderboards: world, this week and your friends
• Playable in English, Dutch, French and German
• No account needed: no e-mail, no name, no age
• Coins can't be bought: everything is earned by playing

Gridquake shows ads. You choose when to watch a reward video (to play on, for coins or for an extra spin); a short ad may appear after every fifth game.
