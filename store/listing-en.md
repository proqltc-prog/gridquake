## Full description (max 4000)
Gridquake is a block puzzle game with a bite. Drag blocks onto the 8×8 grid, fill rows and columns, and watch the lines blow apart. Clear lines move after move, build combos and set the board on fire. Easy to learn, hard to put down: the perfect brain game for a short break or a long puzzle session.

BLOCK PUZZLE CAMPAIGN: 220 LEVELS ON 11 MAPS
Every map brings new puzzles: stones that take several hits, ice, chains, creeping moss, time bombs, islands, paint, keys and chests, rising lava and falling crystals, and a master test that throws it all at you. Some levels start with blocks already on the board in a shape, others run against the clock, and later maps race both moves and time. Earn up to three stars per level and bronze, silver and gold medals per map. Levels get harder step by step, so beginners and puzzle experts both find a good challenge.

CLASSIC BLOCK PUZZLE
No limits, no goals: the classic block puzzle. Keep placing blocks on the grid until the board is full and beat your high score. Plays offline, no Wi-Fi needed.

COMBOS AND POWER BLOCKS
Clear two lines at once and you forge a Rocket. Three at once gives a Bomb. A full line in one colour makes a Prism. Line two power blocks up side by side and they go off together. Keep clearing lines in a row and your combo climbs to FRENZY.

3-MINUTE PVP DUELS
Challenge a random player or a friend in a 3-minute puzzle duel. You both get exactly the same blocks; after three minutes the higher score wins. A live bar shows how your opponent did at the same moment.

HELPERS, COINS AND THE WHEEL OF LUCK
Smash a cell with the Hammer, turn a block, swap your blocks for new ones or drop a Rocket where you need it. Earn coins by winning levels and playing Classic, spin the Wheel of Luck every day and open a gift every third level. A heart lets you play on once per game.

THEMES
Light, Disco, Sunset, Deep sea, Space and Neon night: colourful blocks and lively backgrounds that move, glow and react to your combos. All themes are free.

WHY YOU WILL LIKE THIS PUZZLE GAME
